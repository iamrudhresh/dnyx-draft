# Product Requirements & Roadmap

## 1. Vision
Dnyx Draft is a modern, privacy-first, local-first workspace for viewing, editing, diagramming, and sharing Markdown documents with zero friction.

## 2. Core Personas
- **Software Engineers & DevOps**: Writing READMEs, architecture docs, Mermaid/PlantUML diagrams, and API guides.
- **Researchers & Academics**: Writing notes with complex LaTeX mathematical formulas and data tables.
- **Writers & Note-Takers**: Organizing multi-document workspaces in nested folders with client-side encrypted vaults.

## 3. Key Feature Pillars
1. **Local-First Reliability**: Instant offline readiness, IndexedDB persistence, no accounts needed.
2. **Visual Richness**: Inline rendering of Mermaid, KaTeX, PlantUML, Vega-Lite, STL 3D models, WaveDrom, and ABC notation.
3. **Collaboration & Sharing**: Ephemeral Share Snapshot links and real-time multiplayer Live Share rooms.
